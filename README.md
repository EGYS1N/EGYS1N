I make mods for RimWorld, Minecraft, PEAK and Darkwater, and my own games. Made in Ukraine.

## Released

### RimWorld 1.7, on the Steam Workshop

| Mod | Version | What it does |

| [Seamless World](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3823099600) | 0.8.0, beta | The edge of the map is no longer the end of the world. The six neighbouring tiles become real maps around your colony, and your colonists walk there to hunt, gather or chase traders. |

| [Trodden Trails](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3834878122) | 0.3.0 | Caravans that keep using one route wear a trail into the world map, and the next trip is faster. Nobody built it. You just walked a lot. |

## Coming next

- KFSOS Lights and Wearables (Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge). Two mods from my survival modpack: rechargeable lights for the hand, the head and the gun, and worn items that give effects through simple data rules. Planned for Modrinth.
- Scout mods for PEAK. Three mods that share one scout passport: an item journal that fills in as you find things, paper-doll gear made from vanilla cosmetics, and badges that start as rumours. Planned for Thunderstore.

## In the works

- KFSOS Lite (Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge). The main KFSOS modpack for Minecraft: survival among the ruins, a PDA with its own Network, dangerous expeditions, medicine, weapons, a shelter to build up and layered crafting.
- SCRAPWELD: Vermin to Apex (my own game, Unreal Engine 5). A cyborg tears parts out of enemies in a fight, bolts them onto its own body and changes how the next raid plays.
